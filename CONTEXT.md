# Neon Harbor
An independent courier rebuilds a community radio station in a fictional coastal city.

## Language
**Job**: A contact's ordered set of objectives with a single reward. _Avoid_: quest, heist.
**Stage**: One current objective within a job.
**Heat**: The city's temporary attention level, from zero to three. _Avoid_: wanted stars.
**Patrol**: A road-bound vehicle that follows a courier while heat is active.
**Safehouse**: The courier's apartment and vehicle repair point.
**Recovery**: Returning the courier and loaner car safely to the safehouse.
**Loaner**: The personal drivable coupe available after every recovery.
**Completion**: A permanently rewarded job outcome.
